# Product Manager

Categories for people who decide what gets built and why.

- **Discovery & Research**: Customer interviews, problem framing, validating ideas
- **Strategy & Roadmap**: Vision, roadmap planning, positioning
- **Prioritization**: Trade-offs, scoping, saying no, backlog decisions
- **Stakeholder Management**: Alignment with leadership, sales, support, partners
- **Launch & Delivery**: Specs, working with engineering and design, shipping
- **Metrics & Analytics**: Defining success, measuring outcomes, experiments
- **Leadership & Mentoring**: Leading initiatives, mentoring, influencing without authority
- **Learning & Growth**: New domains, methods, and skills picked up
