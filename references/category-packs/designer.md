# Designer

Categories for people who research and design products and experiences.

- **Research**: User interviews, usability testing, synthesizing insights
- **Interaction Design**: Flows, information architecture, behavior
- **Visual Design**: Layout, typography, color, brand expression
- **Design Systems**: Components, tokens, documentation, consistency
- **Prototyping**: Interactive prototypes, motion, testing ideas quickly
- **Collaboration**: Working with product and engineering, critiques, handoff
- **Leadership & Mentoring**: Leading design efforts, mentoring, raising design quality
- **Learning & Growth**: New tools, methods, and skills picked up
