# General Professional

A starting point for any role. Rename these to fit your work.

- **Core Craft**: The main skills your job is built on
- **Problem Solving**: Untangling hard problems, root causes, creative fixes
- **Communication**: Writing, presenting, explaining clearly
- **Collaboration**: Working well with others, cross-team work
- **Organization & Delivery**: Planning, follow-through, meeting commitments
- **Leadership & Mentoring**: Leading efforts, helping others grow
- **Learning & Growth**: New skills and knowledge picked up
