# Data & Analytics

Categories for people who analyze data and build data products.

- **Analysis**: Exploratory analysis, answering business questions, insight
- **Modeling**: Statistical models, machine learning, forecasting
- **Data Engineering**: Pipelines, data quality, warehousing, transformations
- **Visualization**: Dashboards, charts, data storytelling
- **Experimentation**: A/B tests, experiment design, causal inference
- **Stakeholder Communication**: Translating findings into decisions
- **Leadership & Mentoring**: Leading efforts, mentoring, setting standards
- **Learning & Growth**: New techniques, tools, and domains picked up
