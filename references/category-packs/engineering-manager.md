# Engineering Manager

Categories for people who lead engineering teams.

- **People Development**: Coaching, feedback, growth plans, career conversations
- **Hiring**: Interviewing, sourcing, onboarding new team members
- **Team Health**: Morale, process friction, workload balance, psychological safety
- **Technical Direction**: Architecture guidance, technical strategy, quality bar
- **Delivery**: Planning, execution, removing blockers, shipping on commitments
- **Cross-team Collaboration**: Working with product, design, other teams, stakeholders
- **Leadership & Mentoring**: Leading initiatives beyond the team, mentoring other leaders
- **Learning & Growth**: New management skills and practices picked up
