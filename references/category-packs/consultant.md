# Consultant (context pack)

Stack this with a role pack for people who work on client engagements.

- **Client Communication**: Demos, status updates, managing expectations, building trust
- **Delivery & Process**: Estimates, planning, keeping commitments, adapting process to the client
- **Discovery & Scoping**: Understanding client needs, scoping work, writing proposals
- **Ramp-up on New Domains**: Learning a client's business, systems, and vocabulary quickly
